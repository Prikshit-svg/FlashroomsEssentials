FlashRooms Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FlashRooms Android application ("FlashRooms", "the app", "we") handles information when you use it. By using the app, you acknowledge this policy.

1. About FlashRooms

FlashRooms is an Android application for browsing and searching hotel information. For privacy questions, contact flashroomsadmin@gmail.com.

2. Information we and our service providers process

  • Account information (Firebase Authentication): if you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes the credentials and account identifiers needed to sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • Hotel searches: when you search for hotels or open hotel details, the search terms and request details are sent to our hotel data provider so it can return results. Like any internet request, the provider's servers may also receive technical information such as your IP address.
  • Local app data: the app stores some data on your device in an on-device database (Room). This data stays on your device unless you use a feature that sends it elsewhere. It may be included in Android device backups, depending on your device settings.
  • Advertising and device information: see section 4.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and secure your account.
  • To respond to your searches and display hotel information.
  • To store app data on your device so the app works as expected.
  • To show and measure advertising, and to help prevent ad fraud.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FlashRooms shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and how ads perform, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. Where this applies, you can review or change your choice at any time from the ad privacy settings option in the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings under Google → Ads. For more detail on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services and sharing

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): used for sign-in. See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Hotel data provider, {{HOTEL_API_NAME}}: used to retrieve hotel information. See its privacy policy: {{HOTEL_API_POLICY_URL}}.
  • Google AdMob: used to show advertising, as described in section 4.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the app, and where required by law.

6. Permissions

FlashRooms uses network access to load hotel information and ads. Google's advertising SDK uses the advertising ID permission described in section 4. If the app ever needs another permission, it is requested when the related feature is used, with an explanation.

7. Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, including HTTPS for network requests where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Account data is kept until your account is deleted.
  • Local app data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Data held by third parties (the hotel data provider, Google) is handled under their own policies and retention periods. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete data held by those providers.

Deleting your account. You can delete your FlashRooms account and its associated data from inside the app ({{IN_APP_DELETE_PATH}}), or without opening the app by following the steps on our account deletion page. We process verified requests within 30 days. Temporarily deactivating an account is not the same as deleting it.

9. Your choices and rights

You can use the app without ad personalization by changing your Android advertising settings, and you can manage consent in the app where it applies. Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, the UK or India), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, or to raise a privacy complaint, contact us at the email address below. We aim to respond within a reasonable period.

10. Children's privacy

FlashRooms is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or service providers change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
