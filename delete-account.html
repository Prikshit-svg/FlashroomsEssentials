Delete your FlashRooms account

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your FlashRooms account and the data linked to it. You do not need to reinstall the app to use this page.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open FlashRooms and sign in.
  2. Go to {{IN_APP_DELETE_PATH}}.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to flashroomsadmin@gmail.com with the subject Delete my FlashRooms account.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your FlashRooms account, so we can verify the request.
  3. We will confirm by email once your account has been deleted. We process verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your FlashRooms account, including the sign-in details held by Firebase Authentication.
  • Any other data linked to your account that we store on our servers.

What is not deleted or may be kept

  • Data on your device (the local app database) stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Data held by third parties, such as the hotel data provider and Google (including advertising data), is governed by their own policies. See our Privacy Policy.
  • We may keep limited information where the law requires it, or for security and fraud prevention.
